## Usage

`RandomInfraObserver[g, InfraObserver[p, q], n]` gives n distinct saturated chains from p to q drawn uniformly as vertex lists.

`RandomInfraObserver[g, p, q, n]` and RandomInfraObserver[g, n] are shortcuts.
