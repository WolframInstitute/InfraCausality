## Usage

`KFactor[v]` gives the Bondi factor Sqrt[(1 + v)/(1 - v)], and KFactor[v, phi] its angular form Sqrt[Sin[phi + alpha]/Sin[phi - alpha]] with alpha = WorldlineAngle[v, phi].
