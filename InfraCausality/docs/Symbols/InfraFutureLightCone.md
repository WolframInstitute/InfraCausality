## Usage

<code>[InfraFutureLightCone]()[*v*]</code> is the future light cone of the event *v*: the events whose causal diamond with *v* is a chain, carried by the outgoing light rays of *v*.

## Details & Options

Definition: the future light cone of an event *v* is *E*⁺(*v*) = {*e* ≽ *v* : [*v*, *e*] is a chain}, the events of the causal future that *v* reaches along a single route. Its members, the outgoing light rays of *v*, are the maximal chains of *E*⁺(*v*) that start at *v*.

An event *e* of the causal future lies on the light cone exactly when the diamond [*v*, *e*] has τ(*v*, *e*) + 1 events, no more than one longest chain: nothing can travel from *v* to *e* by a second route, so the separation of *v* and *e* has no spatial extent. This is the discrete analogue of null separation. On the diamond lattice the light cone of {*i*, *j*} is the two null lines through it, the events that share one coordinate with it; on a sprinkling, where the events are in general position, it consists mostly of the links of *v* and is rarely more than a few events deep.

The head holds its event and computes nothing. Its graph, <code>[`InfraMeasurement`](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/ref/InfraMeasurement)[*g*, *c*, "Graph"]</code>, is the Hasse diagram restricted to *E*⁺(*v*): a tree rooted at *v*, since every event of the cone has exactly one predecessor in it. Its root-to-leaf paths are the light rays.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| `"Graph"` | the tree of the light cone, rooted at *v* |
| `"Cardinality"` | the number of outgoing light rays, the leaves of the tree |
| `"Length"` | the distinct numbers of edges of the rays, one integer when they agree |
| `"VertexDensity"` | for each event of the cone, the number of rays through it |
| `"EdgeDensity"` | for each edge of the tree, the number of rays through it |
| `"Faithful"` | `True` |

A member is a vertex list starting at *v*; [RandomInfraFutureLightCone]() draws rays uniformly, and [InfraLightRayQ]() recognises one. The message relation of [InfraMessageFunction]() sends a tick of one observer to the ticks of another that lie on its future light cone.

Corresponding notions:

| System | Name | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| Minkowski space | Future light cone | The boundary of the causal future of *p*, generated by the future-directed null geodesics from *p*. |
| EPS | Light ray | The light rays through an event generate its light cone, which separates the events it can reach by particles from the rest. |

## Basic Examples

The future light cone of an event at height 1, on the diamond lattice, a sprinkling into two-dimensional Minkowski space and the cylinder lattice. On the lattices it is the pair of null lines through the event.

```wl
GraphicsRow @ Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    {v = (SeedRandom[5]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[1]])},
    InfraSubstrateHighlight[g, {InfraFutureLightCone[v], v}]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

The number of light rays and their lengths for the same events.

```wl
Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    {v = (SeedRandom[5]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[1]])},
    Values @ InfraMeasurement[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], {"Cardinality", "Length"}]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

The light cone of the sprinkled event as a tree, beside its causal future.

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "Small"]},
  {v = (SeedRandom[5]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[1]])},
  GraphicsRow[{
    InfraMeasurement[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], "Graph"],
    InfraSubstrateHighlight[g, {InfraCausalFuture[v], InfraFutureLightCone[v], v}]}]]
```

The members are the light rays, vertex lists from *v*.

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "Small"]},
  {v = (SeedRandom[5]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[1]])},
  RandomInfraFutureLightCone[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], All]]
```

## Properties and Relations

An event of the causal future lies on the light cone exactly when its diamond with *v* has τ(*v*, *e*) + 1 events.

```wl
Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    AllTrue[VertexList[g], v |->
      Keys[InfraMeasurement[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], "VertexDensity"]] ===
        Select[RandomInfraCausalFuture[g, v], e |->
          Length[RandomInfraCausalDiamond[g, v, e]] == InfraMeasurement[g, InfraWorldline[v, e], "Length"] + 1]]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

The graph of the light cone is a tree, and every ray passes [InfraLightRayQ]().

```wl
Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    AllTrue[VertexList[g], v |->
      TreeGraphQ[UndirectedGraph @ InfraMeasurement[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], "Graph"]] &&
        InfraLightRayQ[g, RandomInfraFutureLightCone[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], All]]]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

On the diamond lattice the light cone of {*i*, *j*} is the set of events of its causal future that share a coordinate with it.

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["DiamondLatticeGraph", "Small"]},
  AllTrue[VertexList[g], v |->
    Keys[InfraMeasurement[g, InfraFutureLightCone[v], "VertexDensity"]] ===
      Select[RandomInfraCausalFuture[g, v], e |-> First[e] == First[v] || Last[e] == Last[v]]]]
```
