## Usage

`RandomInfraWorldline[g, InfraWorldline[p, q], n]` gives n distinct longest chains from p to q drawn uniformly as vertex lists.

`RandomInfraWorldline[g, p, q, n]` and RandomInfraWorldline[g, n] are shortcuts.
