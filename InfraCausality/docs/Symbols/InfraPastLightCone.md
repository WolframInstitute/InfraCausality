## Usage

<code>[InfraPastLightCone]()[*v*]</code> is the past light cone of the event *v*: the events whose causal diamond with *v* is a chain, carried by the incoming light rays of *v*.

## Details & Options

Definition: the past light cone of an event *v* is *E*⁻(*v*) = {*e* ≼ *v* : [*e*, *v*] is a chain}, the events of the causal past that reach *v* along a single route. Its members, the incoming light rays of *v*, are the maximal chains of *E*⁻(*v*) that end at *v*, listed in causal order.

The past light cone is the time reverse of the future light cone: [InfraPastLightCone]() on a graph is [InfraFutureLightCone]() on the graph with every edge reversed, with each ray read backwards. It holds the events that can send a light signal to *v*; on the diamond lattice these are the two null lines below *v*.

The head holds its event and computes nothing. Its graph, <code>[`InfraMeasurement`](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/ref/InfraMeasurement)[*g*, *c*, "Graph"]</code>, is the Hasse diagram restricted to *E*⁻(*v*), with the edge directions of the graph: a tree whose edges all lead into *v*. Its leaf-to-root paths are the incoming light rays.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| `"Graph"` | the tree of the light cone, every path ending at *v* |
| `"Cardinality"` | the number of incoming light rays |
| `"Length"` | the distinct numbers of edges of the rays, one integer when they agree |
| `"VertexDensity"` | for each event of the cone, the number of rays through it |
| `"EdgeDensity"` | for each edge of the tree, the number of rays through it |
| `"Faithful"` | `True` |

A member is a vertex list ending at *v*; [RandomInfraPastLightCone]() draws rays uniformly, and [InfraLightRayQ]() recognises one.

Corresponding notions:

| System | Name | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| Minkowski space | Past light cone | The boundary of the causal past of *p*, generated by the past-directed null geodesics from *p*: the events *p* sees. |
| EPS | Light ray | The light rays arriving at an event generate its past light cone. |

## Basic Examples

The past light cone of an event one slice below the top, on the diamond lattice, a sprinkling into two-dimensional Minkowski space and the cylinder lattice.

```wl
GraphicsRow @ Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    {v = (SeedRandom[1]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[Max[UniversalTime[g]] - 1]])},
    InfraSubstrateHighlight[g, {InfraPastLightCone[v], v}]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

The number of incoming light rays and their lengths for the same events.

```wl
Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    {v = (SeedRandom[1]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[Max[UniversalTime[g]] - 1]])},
    Values @ InfraMeasurement[g, InfraPastLightCone[v], {"Cardinality", "Length"}]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

The incoming light rays of the sprinkled event, each a vertex list ending at the event.

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "Small"]},
  {v = (SeedRandom[1]; RandomChoice @ RandomInfraSlice[g, InfraSlice[6]])},
  RandomInfraPastLightCone[g, InfraPastLightCone[v], All]]
```

Both light cones of one event of the cylinder lattice. The future cone wraps around the cylinder; the past cone stops at the bottom row.

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["CylinderLatticeGraph", "Small"]},
  InfraSubstrateHighlight[g, {InfraFutureLightCone[{0, 2}], InfraPastLightCone[{0, 2}], {0, 2}}]]
```

## Properties and Relations

The past light cone on a graph is the future light cone on the reversed graph, with every ray read backwards.

```wl
Table[
  With[
    {g = CausalSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
    AllTrue[VertexList[g], v |->
      Sort[RandomInfraPastLightCone[g, InfraPastLightCone[v], All]] ===
        Sort[Reverse /@ RandomInfraFutureLightCone[ReverseGraph[g], InfraFutureLightCone[v], All]]]],
  {name, {"DiamondLatticeGraph", "MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "CylinderLatticeGraph"}}]
```

*e* lies on the past light cone of *v* exactly when *v* lies on the future light cone of *e*.

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["MinkowskiSprinkling2D", "Small"]},
  AllTrue[Tuples[VertexList[g], 2], Apply[{e, v} |->
    KeyExistsQ[InfraMeasurement[g, InfraPastLightCone[v], "VertexDensity"], e] ===
      KeyExistsQ[InfraMeasurement[g, InfraFutureLightCone[e], "VertexDensity"], v]]]]
```

Every incoming ray ends at *v* and passes [InfraLightRayQ]().

```wl
With[
  {g = CausalSubstrate["CylinderLatticeGraph", "Small"]},
  AllTrue[VertexList[g], v |->
    With[{rays = RandomInfraPastLightCone[g, InfraPastLightCone[v], All]},
      AllTrue[rays, ray |-> Last[ray] === v] && InfraLightRayQ[g, rays]]]]
```
